### Project Description

**Classil Keranda Mone** is a completely unnecessary AI-powered classroom door that checks a student's attendance and decides whether they are allowed to enter the class. With reverse attendance logic, students with high attendance may actually be asked to stay outside, while students with low attendance are urgently forced inside.

The project combines a simulated smart door, fake AI diagnostics, teacher personalities, voice announcements, QR scanning, verdict passes, and a useless game hub featuring Ludo, Chess, and Shadow Shift.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)

Students have too much freedom when entering classrooms.

They simply walk into class without the door checking their attendance, judging their behaviour, analyzing the teacher's mood, or giving them unsolicited Malayalam advice.

So we decided to solve the extremely serious problem of **students entering classrooms without being judged by an AI door**.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)

We built an AI-powered classroom door that uses **reverse attendance logic**.

* High attendance → **Door CLOSED**
* Low attendance → **Door OPEN**
* Very low attendance → **EMERGENCY OPEN**
* 100% attendance → **ATTENDANCE OVERLOAD**

The system also checks completely unnecessary parameters such as teacher mood, student behaviour, late entry probability, door mood, and random Malayalam logic before making its final decision.
